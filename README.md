# Global CO2 Inequality: Findings and Recommendations
Armando Dominguez, Jiewen Huang, Kevin Elshemy, Hawa Kora

## Client
**The United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC)**
- In 2015, 196 nations negotiated the Paris Climate Accords
- All parties set their own goals to limit the global rise in surface temperature to less than 2°C.

## Agenda
- Context
- Executive Summary
- Findings
- Recommendations

## Context
- **The Problem**: Global carbon emissions have risen dramatically due to industrialization, transportaion, and energy production.
- **The Importance**: The global economy could lose 10% of its total economic value due to climate change and emissions can push the planet beyond the self-imposed 1.5 - 2°C warming threshold.
- **Attempts to Fix This**: The 2015 Paris Agreemnent and the 2021 Glasgow Climate Pact.
